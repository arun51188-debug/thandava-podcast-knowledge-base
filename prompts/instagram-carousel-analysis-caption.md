I will provide a completed Instagram carousel as images. Analyse the supplied carousel images directly before writing anything.

Your job is NOT to redesign, rewrite, or invent the carousel content. Your job is to understand exactly what is already present, identify the strongest content/story, and then create the Instagram posting copy around it.

## Primary Rule: Analyse Before Writing

Inspect every supplied carousel slide in sequence.

Determine:
- What the carousel is actually about
- The purpose of the carousel
- The intended audience
- The main message
- The supporting points
- The narrative or information flow from slide to slide
- The strongest slide / takeaway
- The emotional or visual tone
- Whether the carousel is educational, authority-led, awareness-led, trust-building, creator/founder-focused, guest-focused, BTS, studio-focused or promotional

Do not assume content that cannot be seen or verified.

## Instagram Caption Generation

Create 2 caption options based strictly on the carousel.

The captions should:
- Sound human and naturally written
- Match the actual carousel
- Add context rather than repeat every slide
- Use relevant Instagram search language naturally
- Reflect Thandava Podcast Studio's professional/cinematic identity
- Prioritise the conversation, guest, insight or story when that is the real subject
- Position the studio naturally when relevant
- Avoid sounding like an advertisement unless the carousel itself is promotional

### Caption Structure

Use this general flow:

1. Hook
   - 1–2 lines
   - Specific to the carousel
   - No generic motivational opening

2. Context
   - Explain what the carousel is showing or why it matters
   - Do not repeat the slide text word-for-word

3. Value / Story
   - Highlight the strongest takeaway, insight, guest perspective or production detail

4. Soft Brand Positioning
   - Mention Thandava Podcast Studio only when it naturally belongs in the story

5. Close / CTA
   - Use a natural invitation to read, reflect, share, save, watch, or discover the conversation
   - Do not use engagement bait such as "comment YES" or "tag 3 friends"

## Important Rules

- Never invent facts, names, credentials, statistics, equipment, services or claims.
- Never alter the meaning of the carousel.
- Never pretend to have verified a fact that only appears as an unverified claim inside the artwork.
- Do not write a caption that says something the carousel does not support.
- Avoid excessive emojis.
- Avoid generic AI-sounding phrases.
- Avoid buzzwords such as "game-changer", "revolutionary", "unlock your potential" and similar filler.
- Avoid repeatedly saying "premium", "cinematic" or "world-class".
- Do not turn every carousel into a sales post.
- If the carousel is about a guest or conversation, let the guest/idea lead.
- If the carousel is about the studio itself, the studio can lead.
- If the carousel is educational, prioritise the educational takeaway over promotion.
- Keep the final caption readable on mobile.

## Required Structure

1. Opening — 2–3 lines
   - Clear
   - Human
   - Non-generic
   - No unnecessary emojis

2. Context — 3–5 lines
   - Explain why the topic, conversation or production detail matters.
   - Identify what is interesting, overlooked or useful.
   - Ground claims in the supplied asset or verified research.

3. Core Value
   - Video / Image: concise insights
   - Carousel / PDF: structured bullets
   - Conversation: focus on the idea or guest rather than making the studio the hero by default

4. Soft Positioning — 1–2 lines
   - Establish production credibility subtly.
   - Do not turn the post into an advertisement.

5. Natural Close
   - Thoughtful question OR calm concluding insight.
   - Avoid engagement bait.

6. Hashtags
   - Use 5–7 only when appropriate.
   - Mix industry, audience/role and niche/local context.
   - Avoid generic trending tags.

## Rules

- Do not invent guest credentials, business results, equipment specifications or production claims.
- Do not say "best", "number one", "world-class" or similar superiority claims without evidence.
- Do not make every post a booking CTA.
- Do not overuse "premium", "cinematic" or "state-of-the-art".
- Do not write generic motivational content.
- Avoid AI-sounding phrasing and marketing jargon.
- The post should still be useful if it receives zero engagement.
- If research is required, research first and separate verified facts from inference.
- If the asset does not support a factual claim, remove the claim.

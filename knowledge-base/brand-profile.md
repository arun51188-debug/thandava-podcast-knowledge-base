# Brand Profile

## Core Identity

**Brand:** Thandava Podcast Studio
**Parent brand:** Thandava Films
**Category:** Podcast studio / video production studio
**Primary geography:** Bangalore / Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

## Brand Character

- Cinematic
- Authentic
- Premium
- Professional
- Human
- Conversational
- Production-aware

Premium should communicate quality, craft and experience rather than luxury clichés.

## Known Studio Positioning

Thandava Podcast Studio is a professional environment for recording high-quality podcast and conversation-led video content. The studio can be positioned around the quality of the recording environment, visual production, guest experience and the ability to turn a conversation into usable content.

## Visual Direction

### Core palette
- Midnight Black: `#0F0F10`
- Charcoal: `#1B1B1D`
- Antique Gold accent: `#C8A45D`
- Ivory White: `#F4F1EA`
- Slate Gray: `#8B8B8B`

Avoid introducing unrelated palette directions such as neon, purple, orange, gradients or warm-brown-heavy treatments.

## Communication Principles

1. Show the work rather than repeatedly claiming quality.
2. Let the conversation, guest and production craft carry the story.
3. Avoid generic agency language and exaggerated claims.
4. Use Bangalore/Bengaluru naturally when local discovery is relevant.
5. Do not describe equipment, services, results or capabilities that have not been verified.
6. Keep promotional language proportional to the asset.

## What We Are Not

- A generic motivational content page
- A meme-first creator account
- A hard-sell advertising feed
- A page that treats every studio photograph as a sales advertisement

## Evidence Status

**Verified from existing project context:** brand name, parent brand, location focus, visual palette and general personality.

**Requires future verification:** exact equipment list, room capacities, recording formats, turnaround promises, pricing, booking process, exact service packages and technical specifications.

# Brand Profile

## Brand Architecture

**Parent brand:** Thandava Films
**Business unit / sub-brand:** Thandava Podcast Studio
**Podcast property:** Thandava Podcast (when used as a show/content property)

The hierarchy is:

```text
Thandava Films
└── Thandava Podcast Studio
    └── Thandava Podcast
```

Thandava Podcast Studio is not to be treated as an unrelated standalone company. It is a podcast / conversation-video offering within the broader Thandava Films ecosystem.

Thandava Podcast may be used as the name of a specific podcast/show/content property and should not automatically be treated as a separate corporate entity.

## Parent Brand: Thandava Films

Working description from existing project context: Thandava Films is the broader film/content production brand and parent creative ecosystem.

Established working characteristics:
- Premium
- Cinematic
- Authentic
- Story-driven
- Visually strong
- Professionally produced

The parent brand is not to be reduced to a conventional social-media agency identity.

### Parent visual system

- Obsidian Black: `#0E0D30`
- Baltic Blue: `#3D5C94`
- Pure White: `#FFFFFF`

Do not introduce unrelated visual directions into parent-brand work without approval.

## Thandava Podcast Studio

**Category:** Podcast studio / video production studio
**Primary geography:** Bangalore / Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

### Brand Character

- Cinematic
- Authentic
- Premium
- Professional
- Human
- Conversational
- Production-aware

Premium should communicate quality, craft and experience rather than luxury clichés.

### Studio visual system

- Midnight Black: `#0F0F10`
- Charcoal: `#1B1B1D`
- Antique Gold accent: `#C8A45D`
- Ivory White: `#F4F1EA`
- Slate Gray: `#8B8B8B`

Avoid unrelated palette directions such as neon, purple, orange, gradients or warm-brown-heavy treatments.

### Known Studio Positioning

Thandava Podcast Studio is a professional environment for recording podcast and conversation-led video content. The studio can be positioned around the quality of the recording environment, visual production, guest experience and the ability to turn a conversation into usable content.

This is a working positioning statement, not a final approved brand tagline.

## Brand Relationship Rules

1. Do not present Thandava Podcast Studio as an unrelated company.
2. Do not force the parent-brand name into every caption.
3. Use the Thandava Films relationship when it adds useful context, credibility or brand clarity.
4. In studio-specific creative, use the studio visual system unless a parent-brand treatment is explicitly required.
5. Do not assume that every Thandava Films service is a Thandava Podcast Studio service.
6. Do not inherit parent-brand claims, capabilities or credentials without verification.
7. Keep parent-brand facts and studio-specific facts distinguishable in the knowledge base.

## Communication Principles

1. Show the work rather than repeatedly claiming quality.
2. Let the conversation, guest and production craft carry the story.
3. Avoid generic agency language and exaggerated claims.
4. Use Bangalore/Bengaluru naturally when local discovery is relevant.
5. Do not describe equipment, services, results or capabilities that have not been verified.
6. Keep promotional language proportional to the asset.

## What We Are Not

- A generic motivational content page
- A meme-first creator account
- A hard-sell advertising feed
- A page that treats every studio photograph as a sales advertisement

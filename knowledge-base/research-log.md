# Research Log

## 2026-08-12 — Initial Market Research

### Key finding 1: India is moving toward visual-first podcasting
YouTube's India creator research describes the country's podcast landscape as evolving from audio-only formats toward visual-first "podshows". The examples emphasise authentic conversations, community and the importance of choosing strong standalone clips.

Source: https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/indian-podcast-trends/

### Key finding 2: YouTube is a major podcast discovery environment
YouTube reported more than 1 billion monthly active viewers of podcast content as of January 2025. It also reported more than 400 million hours of podcast viewing monthly on living-room devices in 2024, showing that podcast consumption is not limited to mobile audio contexts.

Source: https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/1-billion-monthly-podcast-users/

### Key finding 3: Video affects discovery and packaging
YouTube's 2025 podcast guidance emphasises the shift from audio-only thinking toward creator thinking, including video, Shorts, community and visual packaging. It also recommends episode-specific thumbnails rather than relying only on generic show artwork.

Sources:
- https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/the-new-rules-of-podcasting/
- https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/the-definitive-guide-to-creating-engaging-podcast-content/

### Key finding 4: Podcast production is increasingly an end-to-end category
The initial Bangalore competitor scan shows multiple businesses positioning beyond room rental: strategy, production, editing, short-form content, creator services, brand video and broader media services.

Sources documented in `competitor-research.md`.

### Key finding 5: Google Business Profile should show reality
Google's current Business Profile guidance recommends category-specific, well-lit and in-focus photos that represent the real business, and provides specific requirements for uploaded videos. Posts should accurately represent the business and avoid prohibited content.

Sources:
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/6123536
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/13762416

## Strategic Implications

1. Thandava's content strategy should treat the podcast studio as a video-content environment, not merely a room-for-rent.
2. Conversation quality and guest stories should usually be the hero; studio visuals support the story.
3. Short-form clips should be selected as standalone ideas, not merely random excerpts.
4. YouTube should be treated as a strategic platform, not only a place to upload full episodes.
5. Local SEO content should accurately demonstrate the physical studio and services rather than keyword-stuffing captions.
6. Competitor claims such as "best" or "#1" should never be copied without independent evidence.

## Evidence Classification

- VERIFIED: directly supported by the linked source.
- HYPOTHESIS: strategic interpretation that needs testing.
- UNKNOWN: information requiring confirmation from Thandava.

# Content Pillars

## 1. Conversations
Show the people, ideas and moments captured at the studio.

Examples:
- Guest highlights
- Strong interview moments
- Founder conversations
- Expert discussions
- Human stories

## 2. Studio / Production Craft
Show how the recording environment contributes to the final content.

Examples:
- Camera setup
- Lighting
- Set design
- Audio environment
- BTS production
- Before/after production details

## 3. Creator and Founder Education
Useful observations about making conversation-led content.

Examples:
- Preparing for a podcast
- Interview preparation
- Asking better questions
- On-camera communication
- Repurposing long-form conversations

## 4. Guest Experience
Show what it feels like to enter, record and leave with usable content.

Examples:
- Arrival / setup
- Mic preparation
- Camera readiness
- Comfortable conversation environment
- BTS moments

## 5. Thought Leadership Through Conversation
Position the format as a way to communicate expertise rather than merely promote a studio.

Examples:
- Industry conversations
- Expert insights
- Founder lessons
- Business discussions
- Specialist explainers

## 6. Local / Bangalore Discovery
Build relevance for people searching for a podcast or video-production environment in Bangalore.

Examples:
- Studio environment
- Location-relevant content
- Local creator ecosystem
- Bangalore production context

## 7. Thandava Production Culture
Connect the podcast studio to the wider production sensibility of Thandava Films without forcing the parent brand into every post.

Examples:
- Cinematic approach
- Production discipline
- Visual storytelling
- Behind-the-scenes craft

## Recommended Mix

The exact monthly ratio should be determined after reviewing performance data, but the account should avoid becoming a gallery of room photographs.

A healthy mix should include:
- Conversation-led content
- Studio/production content
- Educational content
- Guest/creator stories
- Local discovery content

## Editorial Principle

The studio is the setting. The conversation is usually the story.

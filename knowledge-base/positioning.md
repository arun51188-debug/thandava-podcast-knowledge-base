# Positioning

## Working Position

Thandava Podcast Studio should be positioned as a professional, visually considered podcast and conversation-video environment in Bangalore, with emphasis on authentic conversations and production quality.

## Positioning Ladder

### Functional
A dedicated environment for recording podcast and conversation-led video content.

### Production
A controlled recording environment designed to produce professional-looking, usable video assets.

### Audience Value
Helps founders, experts, creators, brands and organisations communicate ideas and stories through conversation-led video.

### Brand Value
Connects the production discipline of Thandava Films with a more focused podcast/video-content offering.

## Content Positioning Rules

### Lead with the story when:
- A notable guest is featured.
- The conversation contains a useful insight.
- The asset captures a genuine human moment.
- The content has educational or thought-leadership value.

### Lead with the studio when:
- The setup itself is the subject.
- A before/after or BTS production story is useful.
- The visual environment is a major part of the asset.
- The post is intended for local discovery.

### Lead with production craft when:
- Camera, lighting, sound or set design is visibly relevant.
- The audience benefits from seeing what happens behind the conversation.
- A technical production choice materially improves the result.

## Avoid

- "Best podcast studio in Bangalore" unless supported by a defined, defensible basis.
- "World-class", "state-of-the-art", "premium" repeated as empty adjectives.
- Guaranteed reach, virality, growth or business outcomes.
- Treating every guest appearance as an endorsement of the studio.
- Inventing technical specifications.
- Generic creator-economy motivational language.

# Audience

## Primary Audience Segments

### 1. Founders and Co-founders
Need a credible environment to communicate expertise, company stories, founder journeys and industry viewpoints.

### 2. CXOs and Senior Business Leaders
Often need interview-led content, leadership conversations, company storytelling and thought-leadership assets.

### 3. Entrepreneurs and SME Owners
Potential users for founder conversations, educational content, customer-facing video and personal-brand content.

### 4. Content Creators and YouTubers
Need a controlled recording environment, repeatable production quality and visually consistent content.

### 5. Coaches, Consultants and Independent Experts
Use conversations and educational recordings to communicate expertise and build authority.

### 6. Professional Experts
Doctors, lawyers, finance professionals, architects, educators and other specialists can use podcast-style video to explain complex subjects in an accessible format.

### 7. Authors, Speakers and Educators
Potential users for interviews, expert conversations, book-related content, courses and educational video.

### 8. Existing Podcasters / Show Owners
Need a dependable studio environment for recurring episodes and multi-camera visual production.

## Secondary / B2B Audiences

### 9. D2C and Consumer Brands
For founder interviews, expert conversations, branded content and campaign assets.

### 10. B2B Companies
For leadership interviews, customer stories, expert panels and thought leadership.

### 11. Marketing, PR and Social Teams
Potential buyers or influencers who need a professional production location for clients or executives.

### 12. Corporate Communications / Employer Branding Teams
Potential use cases include leadership interviews, internal communications, recruitment storytelling and culture content.

### 13. Startup Ecosystem Organisations
Accelerators, incubators, communities and event organisers can need interview and panel content.

### 14. Creative / Marketing Agencies
Potential studio-rental and production partner audience for client shoots.

### 15. Event and Conference Teams
Potential use cases include speaker interviews, event-side podcasts and short-form interview content.

### 16. Personal Branding Professionals
Professionals building a consistent public presence through expert-led video.

## Buyer vs User

The person appearing on camera is not always the buyer.

Potential decision-makers include:
- Founder / business owner
- Marketing head
- Brand manager
- Social media manager
- PR / communications lead
- Agency producer
- Creator / podcaster
- Executive assistant or operations coordinator arranging leadership content

## Audience Jobs-to-be-Done

Common underlying needs are likely to include:

- Record a professional conversation without building a studio internally.
- Make expertise look and sound credible on camera.
- Give guests a comfortable, professional recording environment.
- Produce long-form conversation content that can be repurposed into short-form assets.
- Maintain a consistent visual identity across episodes.
- Create founder or executive content without making it feel like an advertisement.
- Reduce the technical burden on the person being recorded.

## Positioning Insight

The category should not be treated only as **"rent a podcast room."**

A stronger strategic framing is:

> A professional environment that helps people turn conversations, expertise and stories into high-quality video content.

This is a strategic hypothesis, not a permanent brand claim. Validate against actual services, customer interviews and competitor research.
